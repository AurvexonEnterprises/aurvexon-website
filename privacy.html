Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aurvexon Talent (“we”, “us”, or “our”) may collect, use, store, and protect information submitted through this website and in communications with candidates and employers.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the service used, we may receive information such as name, email address, phone number, location, education and work experience, CV or profile details, job preferences, company and recruiter contact details, vacancy information, and messages sent to us. Please do not provide information that is not relevant to the service requested.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to candidate, employer, and general enquiries.
  • To review candidate profiles and preferences and provide relevant career or recruitment support.
  • To understand employer hiring requirements and communicate about recruitment services.
  • To maintain records, improve our services, protect the website, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing information

We do not intend to sell personal information. Where appropriate and relevant to a candidate’s stated job interests, information may be shared with prospective employers for recruitment consideration. We may also use service providers that help operate our communications, forms, hosting, or business systems, and may disclose information where required by law. We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be held in tools or systems used to operate our services, including form or spreadsheet systems, when those integrations are enabled.

5. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, ongoing recruitment or business communication, record keeping, or legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the service involved.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Children

This website and our recruitment services are intended for people who can lawfully use them for employment-related purposes. Do not submit another person’s information without appropriate authority.

8. Third-party services and links

The website may link to or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage users to review those policies when using external services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
